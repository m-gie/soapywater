description=[h2]Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Add soap[/b]: right-click any container holding water (clean or tainted) and choose [i]Add soap[/i]. Every started litre takes 1 soap use. Soap comes from your emptiest bars first, bags included. If you're short, the option tells you how many uses you need.
description=[*][b]Clean Stains (Soapy Water)[/b]: with a mop, broom, sponge, bath towel, dish cloth, grill brush or toilet brush, pick stained floor or wall tiles just like with bleach. Each tile uses 0.5 L.
description=[*]Bleach and cleaning liquid still work exactly as in vanilla.
description=[*][b]Don't drink it.[/b] Soapy water makes you sick.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Good to know[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Build 42, single player and multiplayer. The server and every player need the mod.
description=[*]Lua and scripts only, no Java.
description=[*]Can be added to an existing save.
description=[*]English and Polish.
description=[/list]
